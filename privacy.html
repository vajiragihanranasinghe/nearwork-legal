NearWork — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

NearWork ("we", "our", "the app") is a classifieds and job platform for Sri Lanka. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account info: email address, display name, profile photo (optional).
  • Content you post: ads, photos, chat messages, job posts, ratings.
  • Location: approximate location when you use search or post ads with a location.
  • Device info: device model, OS version, app version, crash logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the NearWork service (create account, post ads, chat, search).
  • To improve the app, fix bugs, and prevent abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate NearWork:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, push notifications) — Firebase Privacy
  • Supabase (image storage) — Supabase Privacy
  • Google Maps / Places (location search) — Google Privacy

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with the service providers listed above, or when legally required.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app, or by emailing vajiragihanranasinghe@gmail.com. We delete your account data within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

NearWork is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit and follow Firebase/Supabase security best practices for data at rest.

8. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be shown in-app.
