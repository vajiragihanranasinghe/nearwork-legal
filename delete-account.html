Delete Your NearWork Account

You can delete your NearWork account and all associated data directly from the app:

In-app: Open NearWork → Settings → Delete Account → type DELETE → confirm.

If you cannot access the app (lost device, forgotten password, etc.), email us and we will process the deletion within 7 days:

Email: wajira4u@gmail.com
Subject: Delete Account
Include: the email address associated with your account

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, phone, email)
  • All ads you have posted, including uploaded images
  • Your blocked-users list and other preferences
  • Your account credentials (Firebase Authentication record)

Deletion is immediate when done from the app. Email requests are processed within 7 days.

Some data may be retained for a limited period as required by law or for fraud prevention, as described in our Privacy Policy.
