Delete Your NearWork Account

You can delete your NearWork account in two ways:

Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

  1. Open NearWork
  2. Tap Settings in the bottom menu
  3. Scroll to Account
  4. Tap 🗑️ Delete My Account
  5. Confirm

Option 2 — By email

Send an email to vajiragihanranasinghe@gmail.com with subject "Delete Account" and include the email address associated with your NearWork account.

Request Account Deletion

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, photo)
  • All your ads
  • All your chat messages
  • All your favorites, ratings, and job alerts

Timeframe

We delete your data within 30 days of your request. Some anonymized data may be retained for legal and fraud-prevention purposes.
